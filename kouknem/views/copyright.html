1. Nature of the Service

Kouknem provides technology that enables users to search for, locate, reference and interact with information and video resources available through third-party services on the Internet.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, Kouknem does not claim ownership of third-party audiovisual works displayed, referenced, indexed or otherwise made discoverable through the Service.

Third-party resources remain under the control and responsibility of their respective providers and, where applicable, the persons who made those resources available.

The appearance of a search result, link, reference or third-party resource within the Service does not constitute a representation that Kouknem owns, licenses, endorses or has independently verified the legal status of that resource.

2. Respect for Intellectual Property Rights

We respect copyright and other intellectual property rights.

Users may not use the Service to knowingly submit, distribute, promote or facilitate access to material in violation of applicable intellectual property law or our Terms of Service.

Rights holders and their authorised representatives may notify us about specific material they believe infringes their rights.

We review sufficiently precise and substantiated notices and take appropriate action where required.

3. Notice-and-Action Procedure

A person or entity may submit a notice regarding allegedly illegal or infringing material accessible through the Service.

Notices should be sent through:

  • Copyright form: {{REPORT_URL}}
  • or Email: {{COPYRIGHT_EMAIL}}

To allow us to properly assess a notice, it should contain:

  1. the full name of the notifying person or entity;
  2. a valid email address;
  3. identification of the copyrighted work or other legal right concerned;
  4. identification of the allegedly infringing or illegal material;
  5. the exact URL or other information allowing us to precisely locate the relevant result or resource within the Service;
  6. a reasonably detailed explanation of why the material is considered unlawful;
  7. where the notice is submitted on behalf of another person or organisation, confirmation of the authority to act on their behalf;
  8. a statement confirming that the information supplied is accurate to the best of the notifier's knowledge and submitted in good faith.

We may request additional information where a notice is insufficiently precise to allow us to identify or assess the material concerned.

4. Processing of Notices

Upon receipt of a sufficiently precise and adequately substantiated notice, we will assess it without undue delay.

Depending on the circumstances, we may:

  • disable access to a specific result;
  • remove a specific result from our index;
  • disable playback or other functionality associated with the identified resource;
  • prevent a specific URL from being re-indexed;
  • restrict or suspend a user responsible for repeated violations;
  • request additional evidence from the notifying party;
  • notify an affected user where appropriate;
  • preserve relevant records where reasonably necessary for legal or compliance purposes;
  • take any other proportionate action required by applicable law.

The submission of a notice does not automatically establish that the reported material is unlawful.

Each sufficiently substantiated notice is assessed on its individual circumstances.

5. Knowledge of Illegal Content

We do not assume that every resource available through a third-party service is unlawful merely because it has been reported or indexed.

However, where we obtain sufficiently specific information allowing us to identify particular illegal content or activity, we will act expeditiously where required by applicable law.

Where appropriate, we may temporarily restrict access while a notice is being assessed.

6. Re-Indexing Prevention

Where a specific URL has been removed or disabled following a valid infringement notice, we may record a technical identifier for that URL to prevent the same resource from being automatically re-indexed.

Removal of one URL does not necessarily establish that all other versions of the same work are unlawful.

Rights holders should identify additional locations where reasonably necessary.

7. Repeat Infringement

Users must not repeatedly submit or facilitate access to resources that violate copyright or other applicable law.

Where we reasonably determine that a user is repeatedly responsible for substantiated infringements, we may apply measures including:

  • warnings;
  • temporary restrictions;
  • removal of submitted resources;
  • suspension of functionality;
  • account suspension;
  • permanent account termination.

Measures are determined according to the circumstances and proportionality of the violation.

8. Misuse of the Reporting System

The notice-and-action system must not be used to knowingly submit false, misleading or abusive reports.

Persons submitting notices should have a reasonable basis for asserting that the identified material is unlawful.

We reserve the right to disregard manifestly unfounded or abusive notices and to take appropriate measures against repeated misuse of the reporting mechanism where permitted by applicable law.

9. Third-Party Services

Some functionality of the Service may involve resources or information originating from independently operated third-party websites or services.

We do not control the operation, availability, content policies or legal compliance of those third parties.

Where a resource is hosted by a third party, removing it from our Service does not necessarily remove the original material from the Internet.

Rights holders may therefore also wish to contact the operator hosting the underlying material.

10. No Ownership Claim

Unless expressly identified as our own content, the inclusion of a title, thumbnail, metadata, hyperlink, search result or other reference within the Service does not imply ownership by Kouknem.

All trademarks, titles and copyrighted works remain the property of their respective owners.

11. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of the Service complies with applicable law and that they possess any rights or permissions required for material they submit.

Users must not intentionally:

  • provide links to material they know is unlawfully distributed;
  • circumvent access controls or technological protection measures;
  • submit misleading information regarding ownership or licensing;
  • repeatedly resubmit resources previously removed for legal reasons;
  • use the Service primarily for unlawful distribution of protected content.

12. Cooperation With Rights Holders and Authorities

Valid and binding orders requiring removal, restriction or disclosure will be processed in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

We also welcome structured communication with copyright owners, distributors, studios and authorised anti-piracy representatives where they need to report multiple specific resources.
